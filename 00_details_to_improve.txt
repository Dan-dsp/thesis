THESIS OBJECTIVES
Source: 02_thesis_document/main.tex, lines 445-463.
General: Develop a system for classifying representative bird species of
southwestern Colombia in digital images using Machine Learning.
Specific objectives:
1. Build a public-source image dataset for training and testing.
2. Apply filtering, enhancement and segmentation to highlight bird attributes,
   facilitate localization and improve differentiation between species.
3. Implement Shallow Learning while addressing underfitting and overfitting.
4. Implement Deep Learning while addressing underfitting and overfitting.
5. Establish the scope and limitations of both recognition systems.
